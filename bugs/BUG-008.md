# BUG-008: Form labels are not linked to their inputs (accessibility)

- **Reported by:** Hiruni Nimeshika
- **Observed:** 17 November 2026
- **Area:** Accessibility
- **Severity:** Low
- **Priority:** Low
- **Status:** Fixed in code (found in code review), retest needed
- **Environment:** Source review of `src/App.jsx` (earlier version).

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the alert form
2. Click the label text 'Email Address' or 'Target Price ($)'

## Expected result
Clicking a label focuses its input, and screen readers announce the label with the field.

## Actual result
The labels have no `htmlFor` and the inputs have no `id`, so they are not associated.

## Evidence
- Code review of the alert form markup.

## Root cause
Missing `htmlFor` and `id` attributes.

## Fix
Added matching `id` and `htmlFor` for the email, coin and price fields.

Click each label and confirm its field receives focus.
