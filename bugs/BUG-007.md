# BUG-007: Prices are shown without thousands separators, for example '$64563.00'

- **Reported by:** Hiruni Nimeshika
- **Observed:** 17 November 2026
- **Area:** UI / formatting
- **Severity:** Low
- **Priority:** Low
- **Status:** Open (verify)
- **Environment:** Google Chrome, Windows, http://localhost:5173

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the app and look at the Bitcoin price

## Expected result
Large prices are easy to read, for example '$64,563.00'.

## Actual result
Bitcoin shows '$64563.00' and Ethereum '$1816.77'.

## Evidence
- ![evidence](../../screenshots/BUG-003-local-identical-hardcoded-change-2026-11-17-0754.png)

## Root cause
Prices are formatted with `toFixed(2)` only.

## Fix
Use `toLocaleString('en-US', { minimumFractionDigits: 2, maximumFractionDigits: 2 })`.
