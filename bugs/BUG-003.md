# BUG-003: All coins show the same hard-coded '+2.4%' change and the same price bar

- **Reported by:** Hiruni Nimeshika
- **Observed:** 17 November 2026
- **Area:** Data accuracy
- **Severity:** High
- **Priority:** High
- **Status:** Fixed in code, live retest needed
- **Environment:** Google Chrome, Windows, http://localhost:5173 (local build).

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the local app and load the five coins
2. Compare the percentage badge on each card

## Expected result
Each coin shows its own real 24-hour price change, green when positive and red when negative. The bar reflects the data.

## Actual result
Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Cardano and Polkadot all show '+2.4%', and every bar is the same length. Real 24-hour changes are never identical for five coins. This is fake data presented as real.

## Evidence
- ![evidence](../../screenshots/BUG-003-local-identical-hardcoded-change-2026-11-17-0754.png)

## Root cause
The percentage was written directly in the component (`<div className="price-change">+2.4%</div>`) and the bar width was fixed at 65%.

## Fix
The app now requests `include_24hr_change=true` from CoinGecko and displays `usd_24h_change`, coloured green or red, and scales the bar width from the change.

## Retest
Open the live site and confirm the five percentages differ and match CoinGecko's 24-hour values, and that a negative change is red.
